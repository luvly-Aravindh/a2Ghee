Not Bilona-Style. Real Bilona.

Much of the ghee on store shelves is made by heating cream. Bilona starts with whole milk set into curd. The curd is churned until the butter separates, and the butter is slow-cooked on a low flame.

It takes longer and gives far less ghee per litre of milk. That is exactly why it tastes different.

Common Questions and Concerns?

What does A2 mean on this ghee?

It means the ghee is made from the milk of indigenous Gir cows, a breed known to produce milk with A2 beta-casein protein. A2 describes the milk source. Ghee itself contains only trace amounts of protein.

What is the Bilona method?

Fresh curd is churned to extract butter, which is then gently heated over a low flame to obtain ghee. It is slow, small-batch work that keeps the natural aroma, grainy texture and authentic taste.

Are there any preservatives or additives?

No. Eliqo A2 ghee is made without chemicals, additives or preservatives. It contains milk, so please avoid it if you have a milk allergy.

How do I store it and how long does it last?

Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat, and use a clean, dry spoon. Best before 12 months from the date of packaging.
